\section{Conclusion}

TRACE-C provides a compact, causal way to ask whether multiple ordinary-looking
telemetry streams form an unusual relational pattern. Its useful contribution
is the complete protocol: robust magnitude-preserving marginals, dependence and
temporal channels, strictly-prior rank calibration, disclosed selection
fallbacks, and a frozen hold-out with provenance.

The results argue for calibrated diagnostics and human review rather than
overstated guarantees. TRACE-C is strongest on coherent multi-stream episodes;
simple reconstruction is stronger on the short frequency transient in this
aggregation. Future work should validate calendar-aware budgets and selection
rules on a subsequent untouched year, and should evaluate dependence-robust
conformal procedures designed for longer-range telemetry dependence.
