TRACE-C is a strictly-prior rank-calibrated detector for aligned operational
telemetry. Same-regime rolling median/MAD residuals feed three window channels:
a maximum normalized local sum, a Gaussian copula-form dependence contrast on
robust-z residuals, and a worst standardized AR(1) innovation. Channel ranks
are Fisher-aggregated and ranked against earlier aggregates. The resulting
$p$-values are selection quantities, not event probabilities. The copula-form
channel is not a literal copula density: the method applies no
probability-integral or normal-score transform.

We evaluate six Great Britain grid streams with a January--April 2019 fit,
July--December 2019 development evidence, and a frozen 2020 hold-out. TRACE-C
ranks Storm Atiyah first among 2019 test windows, but a development-year
channel ablation shows that this is not copula detection: copula-only ranks
Atiyah 59th, dropping the copula channel leaves it 2nd without a record-rule
alert, and the full detector's Atiyah lead channel is local. The short 9 August
frequency event is rank 143 under the fused detector and 40th on the temporal
channel alone; reconstruction baselines rank it first. In 2020 no window is
selected. Empirical rank counts are diagnostics, not coverage or FDR proofs.
