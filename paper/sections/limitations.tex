\section{Limitations and honesty ledger}

The evidence does not establish nominal FDR or coverage for non-exchangeable
grid telemetry. A fixed calibration experiment produced seasonal-drift
artifacts and approximately 20-fold anti-conservative conformal values; the
rolling calibration is the documented correction, with its empirical checks
retained. The parameter sweep and marginal choice used the 2019 development
evidence, and the frequency stream was event-informed. The 2020 hold-out limits
but cannot erase that selection history.

The sensor set and aggregation resolution define what can be detected. A
40-minute transient can disappear in half-hourly summaries, while a broad
weather episode can rank highly because several streams move together. The
fixed two-alert budget groups 48-row blocks and does not perfectly represent
46/50-period daylight-saving days. Finally, baseline training and comparison
were post-hoc; their ranks should not be read as an unbiased leaderboard.
