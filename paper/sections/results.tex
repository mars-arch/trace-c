\section{Results}

The committed evidence contains 2,208 scored windows in 2019 and 4,392 in
2020. In 2019, the empirical rank checks are 120/110 at $p\leq .05$, 51/44 at
$p\leq .02$, and 23/22 at $p\leq .01$ (observed/reference). On the blind
2020 pre-COVID segment the corresponding checks are 50/45 and 3/9 at .05 and
.01, respectively. These are descriptive diagnostics for a drifting,
autocorrelated series, not formal null certification.

Storm Atiyah is rank 1 of 2,208 in 2019 and is alerted. The 9 August 2019 GB
frequency event is in the top approximately 6.5 percent but is not separable at
30-minute aggregation. On 2020, Storm Ciara is rank 44, the lockdown
transition's best window is rank 45, and Storm Dennis is rank 137. The highest
ranked 2020 windows later receive the external interpretations Storm Ellen and
Storm Alex; those labels were not supplied to the detector.

The post-hoc baselines make the trade-off visible. Convolutional autoencoder
and PCA reconstruction rank the short blackout first, Isolation Forest ranks
the lockdown interval first, and TRACE-C ranks Atiyah first while retaining the
most stable-looking rank counts in the supplied comparison. The baseline table
must be regenerated from the JSON reports rather than hand edited.
