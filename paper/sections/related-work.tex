\section{Related work}

Conformal methods obtain finite-sample rank guarantees under exchangeability
\citep{shafer2008tutorial}. For dependent observations, block and robust
variants replace the literal permutation argument with assumptions about the
dependence structure \citep{chernozhukov2018dependentconformal}. Outlier testing
with conformal $p$-values makes the multiple-testing connection explicit and
establishes useful dependence-aware FDR results \citep{bates2023conformaloutliers}.
TRACE-C uses the rank construction as a diagnostic and states where telemetry
violates its idealized conditions.

The copula layer follows the separation of marginals and dependence introduced
by Sklar's theorem \citep{sklar1959fonctions}; Gaussian-copula likelihoods give
a compact relational score for the residual vector \citep{song2000gaussiancopula}.
Fisher's combination statistic \citep{fisher1932statisticalmethods} and the
Benjamini--Hochberg procedure \citep{benjamini1995fdr} are standard ways to
combine evidence and select discoveries. Positive dependence and calibration
reuse matter for the latter, motivating the explicit reference to the
Benjamini--Yekutieli correction literature \citep{benjamini2001dependency}.

The record rule is a transparent finite-history alternative to a nominal tail
threshold; its expected count follows the classical record-value calculation
\citep{renyi1962records}. Robust MAD scaling follows \citet{rousseeuw1993mad}.
For comparison, we include a convolutional autoencoder
\citep{sakurada2014autoencoders,vijay2020kerasautoencoder}, PCA reconstruction
residuals \citep{jackson1979pcaresiduals}, Isolation Forest
\citep{liu2008isolationforest}, and Spectral Residual
\citep{ren2019spectralresidual}.
