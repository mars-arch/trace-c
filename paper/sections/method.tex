\section{Method}

Let $x_{t,j}$ be the aligned value of stream $j$ at half-hour index $t$.
For each regime and stream, TRACE-C computes a strictly-prior rolling median
$m_{t,j}$ and median absolute deviation $d_{t,j}$, then forms
\[
 z_{t,j}=\operatorname{clip}\!\left(\frac{x_{t,j}-m_{t,j}}
 {1.4826d_{t,j}},-10,10\right).
\]
The initial fit period supplies a covariance estimate for the copula channel;
no future window contributes to a score or its reference set.

For a non-overlapping window of width $W$, three channels are computed: a local
aggregate based on the largest normalized stream sum, a Gaussian-copula
dependence surprise based on the residual vector, and the worst AR(1)
innovation. Each channel score is converted to a rank-$p$ against the trailing
window history. With channel $p$-values $p_1,\ldots,p_K$, the combined score is
\[
 S=-2\sum_{k=1}^{K}\log p_k,
 \qquad p_t=\frac{1+\#\{S_s\ge S_t:s<t\}}{n_{t-1}+1}.
\]
The finite-sample rank formula is retained exactly; no decimal rounding is
used in selection.

The primary selection attempts BH at $q=0.05$. If conformal granularity
selects no windows, TRACE-C records a fallback record rule, in which a window
must beat every strictly prior combined score. A hard two-alert daily budget
then limits operator attention. Reports include the rule that actually ran and
the exchangeable-null reference count $\sum_s1/(n_s+1)$; this count is a
reference diagnostic, not a coverage theorem for seasonal telemetry.
