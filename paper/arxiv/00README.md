# arXiv bundle placeholder

The arXiv submission bundle will contain the compiled `main.tex` source,
`references.bib`, all files under `sections/`, generated tables under
`generated/`, and any figures placed under `figures/`. Before submission, add
the public GitHub URL, Zenodo preprint DOI (`10.5281/zenodo.22012123`), and
evidence DOI (`10.57967/hf/10063`) to the availability statement. Verify that
the bundle compiles from a clean checkout. The arXiv identifier is filled
after acceptance.
